How It Works

From browsing to delivery, here's what to expect.

1

Browse Available Listings

Every listing includes real photos, age, weight, sex, breed, registration info, and vaccination/worming records so you know exactly what you're getting.

2

Reserve & Pay with CashApp

Submit your shipping and contact info to reserve the listing. You'll be shown our CashApp $cashtag along with a short reference code — include that code in your CashApp payment note so we can match it to your order.

3

Let Us Know You've Paid

Once you've sent payment, click "I've Sent Payment" on your order. Your reservation stays held while we verify the payment on our end.

4

We Confirm Your Payment

Our team checks CashApp for your payment and confirms your order. You'll get a confirmation once that's done — this isn't always instant, so please be patient.

5

Delivery

We arrange ground transportation through our delivery partner, Puppy Travelers, and coordinate a pickup time and location near you.

  • You'll need to meet the delivery vehicle at a designated time and location reasonably close to your address.
  • Pets cannot be delivered until they're at least 8 weeks old.
  • We deliver to most of the lower 48 United States.
Questions about a delivery already in progress? Call Puppy Travelers directly at 417-451-2252 or 417-622-0359.

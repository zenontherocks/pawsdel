About Paws Delivered

Paws Delivered connects buyers with available puppy and kitten listings and handles the logistics of getting them safely to their new homes.

We list detailed, verified information for every listing — photos, age, weight, sex, breed, registration, and vaccination/worming records — so you can make an informed decision before you buy.

Once your order is confirmed, we arrange ground transportation through our delivery partner, Puppy Travelers, coordinating a pickup time and location close to you.

Checkout is simple: no accounts, no complicated forms — just your shipping details and a CashApp payment.

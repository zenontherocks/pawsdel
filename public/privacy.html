Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Information We Collect

We don't require an account to use Paws Delivered. When you place an order, we collect the shipping and contact information you provide: name, email, phone number, and shipping address. This information is used solely to fulfill your order and coordinate delivery.

Payment Information

We do not process or store any payment card or bank information. Payments are made directly through CashApp between you and us; we only see the payment reference code you're asked to include in your CashApp payment note.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your order
  • To coordinate delivery with our transportation partner, Puppy Travelers
  • To send you order confirmations and delivery updates

Information Sharing

We share your shipping and contact information with our delivery partner, Puppy Travelers, solely for the purpose of completing your delivery. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Data Retention

We retain order records, including buyer information, for completed (paid) orders for our own record-keeping and legal purposes. We do not retain contact information from abandoned or expired orders beyond what's needed to operate the site.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to us via our contact page.
